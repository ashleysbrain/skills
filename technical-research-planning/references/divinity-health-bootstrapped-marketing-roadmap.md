# Divinity Health Bootstrapped Marketing Roadmap Pattern

Session-derived notes for open-core health products where the founders have low marketing experience, low budget, and low energy.

## Core pattern
- Do **not** bury marketing inside generic distribution language when the user asks for market impact.
- Promote marketing to a first-class roadmap phase or board lane.
- Keep the system small enough to execute with low motivation.

## Minimum viable marketing system
Use a one-message, one-audience, one-weekly-block rhythm:
- **One message:** the same core thesis across all channels.
- **One audience at a time:** do not broaden until one wedge has proof.
- **One weekly marketing block:** batch the heavy work once per week.
- **One daily micro-action:** a tiny action on off-days so the system never fully stops.
- **One proof asset per week:** demo clip, case note, screenshot, testimonial, or metric.
- **One reuse rule:** every asset must be repurposed at least once.
- **One feedback loop:** track which messages, assets, and channels produce replies or signups.

## Bootstrap-first launch assets
Create these in the first pass:
- landing page
- waitlist page
- founder story
- demo video
- launch emails
- proof library
- README
- pricing page
- partner deck
- 30-day content calendar

## Open-core boundary
- Community standard: the method, definitions, and baseline workflow.
- Premium moat: hosted intelligence, orchestration, premium assets, and enterprise workflows.
- Revenue strategy belongs in the architecture, not as an afterthought.

## Source-first workflow for roadmap/visual redo
1. Rewrite the source markdown first.
2. Regenerate HTML/PDF derivatives from the source.
3. Verify the exported artifact, not just the layout.
4. Check the exact phrases that matter to the release story.
5. For print-safe board visuals, keep the design monochrome-friendly and compress the last section first if overflow appears.
6. Verify the final PDF is exactly one page when the board is intended as a whiteboard.
