# Divinity Health Pitch-Pack Release Pattern

Use this note when turning Divinity Science research into a Divinity Health master brief, appendix, roadmap, or launch pack.

## Release rule
- Put the Divinity Science vs Divinity Health split on page one.
- Use the exact plain-English boundary:
  - Divinity Science = research, methodology, standards
  - Divinity Health = product, UX, revenue/commercial engine
- Repeat the split in the appendix title page and first opener so nobody has to infer it.

## Source-first workflow
1. Update the source markdown / HTML / generator first.
2. Regenerate the PDFs from source.
3. Verify extracted text, not just the visual render.
4. Confirm the exact opening phrases appear in the output.
5. Send the verified artifact(s) to the user.

## Verification checklist
- Master brief opener states the Science → Health relationship.
- Appendix opener states who owns methodology vs product execution.
- The combined PDF set is what gets delivered, not just screenshots or a description.
- If the user asks to "revise and resend," resend the regenerated artifacts immediately after verification.

## Pitfalls
- Don't patch the PDF directly if the source generator exists.
- Don't bury the brand boundary in a later section.
- Don't say the docs are updated until the regenerated artifact has been verified.
- Don't keep the science layer and product layer vague; the language must be explicit enough for engineers, product, and revenue leads to align quickly.
