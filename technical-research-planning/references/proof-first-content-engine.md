# Proof-First Content Engine (session note)

Session-derived pattern for open-core health-tech marketing:

## Core sequence
1. Collect proof before publishing claims.
2. Classify content by trust level:
   - Tier 1: proof content (screenshots, demos, outcomes)
   - Tier 2: expert interpretation
   - Tier 3: AI-assisted packaging/reformatting
   - Tier 4: synthetic filler (avoid)
3. Turn one proof event into many assets:
   - long post
   - short posts
   - email
   - landing page block
   - partner pitch
   - FAQ
   - demo script
4. Keep the founder voice central; use AI for distribution/repurposing, not as the source of truth.

## Practical guardrails
- Every claim should point to visible evidence.
- If content could be posted by any startup, it's too generic.
- Marketing and research should share the same proof vault.
- Open-source the standard/spec; monetize the hosted intelligence and premium UX.

## Use cases
- Weekly content batching
- Launch asset creation
- Founder-led distribution
- Partner outreach
- Waitlist conversion
