# Divinity Health open-core + avatar commercialization brief

Session-derived notes for future research and planning.

## Strategic shape
- Parent research/IP entity: Divinity Science (kept distinct from product commercialization).
- Commercial product entity: Divinity Health.
- Goal: release a community standard around the 8D methodology and avatar framework while keeping premium intelligence, hosted orchestration, and enterprise workflows proprietary.

## Open vs closed boundary
Open-source / standardize:
- Core 8D framework definitions
- Public schemas for avatar state and wellness dimensions
- Telegram-first interaction patterns
- Community-friendly docs, examples, and starter integrations

Keep proprietary / monetizable:
- Predictive intelligence and adaptive ranking
- Premium orchestration and hosted workflows
- Enterprise dashboards, admin controls, and white-label layers
- Curated avatar assets, cosmetics, and premium experiences

## Avatar rules captured
- Personal avatar: must be human and directly represent the user; it functions as a private healing/functional mirror.
- Public avatar: may be creature-like, stylized, or authentic-human, depending on the social context.
- Do not collapse personal and public identity into one model; treat them as separate product surfaces and data objects.

## Distribution and retention
- Prefer Telegram-first bootstrapping for low-friction check-ins, quests, and streak loops.
- Use healthy retention mechanics only: streaks, progress, adaptive difficulty, and cohort rewards.
- Avoid shame, coercion, or addiction-style engagement loops.

## Monetization ladder
1. Free community standard / open docs
2. Paid hosted consumer product
3. Premium insights / coaching / intelligence layer
4. Enterprise APIs and white-label offerings
5. Implementation, advisory, and customization services

## Research/planning notes
- When packaging methodology for release, explicitly separate the community standard from the premium moat before recommending what to open-source.
- For health-tech products with avatars, treat identity semantics as architecture, not just visual design.
- If business/organizational avatars are included, map the same 8D lens to departments, teams, and AI agents with separate schemas.
- Add a revenue analysis to the research output whenever the product includes a community-facing standard, so monetization is designed alongside architecture.
