# Open-Core Avatar Product Strategy Notes

Session-digest for research/planning work on Divinity Health-style avatar systems and methodology release.

## Durable design takeaways
- Private healing avatar must be human and directly represent the user.
- Public/social avatar must be separate and opt-in.
- Public avatar may be creature-like, symbolic, hybrid, or authentic human.
- Treat the personal/public split as a core product boundary, not a cosmetic choice.
- Telegram is a strong bootstrap channel for low-friction habit loops and distribution.

## Open-core boundary pattern
- Open-source the standard/methodology layer to establish category trust and ecosystem adoption.
- Keep predictive intelligence, orchestration, premium assets, and enterprise workflows proprietary/hosted.
- Monetize expression, access, automation, and scale — not coercive data extraction.

## Research checklist when revisiting this topic
- Identify which layer becomes the public standard.
- Identify which layer is the premium moat.
- Verify privacy/consent flows for avatar state and social sharing.
- Document how health-safe gamification avoids shame and addiction mechanics.
- Make the brand/entity split explicit when a parent lab and commercial subsidiary both exist.
