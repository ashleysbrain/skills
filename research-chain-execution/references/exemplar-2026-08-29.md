## Two-Factor Liveness Proof

When verifying a daemon is truly live (vs frozen-rendering): health endpoint shows SAME stagnant entity-IDs across checks AND advancing counters (pokes/cycles/uptime). Stagnant IDs + advancing counters = live; stagnant IDs + stagnant counters = frozen. Applied to conduction broker :port with approval IDs 5780/6624/6689.
