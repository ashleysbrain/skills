## Body-System Quick Reference

### Nervous System (Brain / Neurotransmitters)
- Four primary neurotransmitters: acetylcholine (focus/learning), dopamine (motivation/reward), serotonin (mood, 90% gut-produced), GABA (calm/parasympathetic)
- Optimization: precursor amino acids (L-tyrosine, L-tryptophan, alpha-GPC), adaptogens (Rhodiola, ashwagandha KSM-66, Bacopa, lion's mane), sensory environment management
- Key protocol: morning nootropic stack + neurotransmitter cycling (stimulatory AM, inhibitory PM)
- "Leaky brain" = blood-brain barrier permeability from systemic inflammation. Fix: omega-3, curcumin, glutathione
- AI transfer: Sherman routing = acetylcholine/dopamine balance. Leaky brain = porous tool boundaries

### Endocrine System (Hormones)
- Key hormones: testosterone, thyroid (T3/T4), cortisol, DHEA, growth hormone, insulin
- Optimization: cold exposure (NE 200-300%, testosterone boost), sleep (T produced during deep sleep), red light 600-950nm (testosterone via Leydig cells), fasting (GH up to 5x at 48hr)
- Hormones are SLOW broadcast signals. Cascade feedback loops. Endocrine disruption is upstream of nearly everything.
- AI transfer: Coupling matrix = hormone cascades. Free-energy gate = endocrine modulator. Thyroid = compute budgeting.

### Cardiovascular (Heart, HRV, VO2 Max)
- HRV = THE primary autonomic balance biomarker. High = parasympathetic (rest/repair). Low = sympathetic (fight/flight).
- VO2 max = single strongest longevity predictor
- Zone 2 (60-70% HR max, 3-4hr/week): builds mitochondrial density via PGC-1alpha. The BASE LAYER.
- VO2 max intervals (1-2hr/week): Norwegian 4x4 protocol (4x4min @ 90-95% with 3min recovery)
- Biomarkers: HRV (RMSSD, LF/HF), resting HR trend, VO2 max, BP, lipid panel (apoB, Lp(a))
- AI transfer: HRV = dimension score volatility. VO2 max = burst capacity ceiling. Zone 2 = background steady-state.

### Mitochondrial / Energy Production
- ATP via oxidative phosphorylation. NAD+ is the electron carrier (declines ~50% by age 50)
- Red/near-infrared light (600-950nm): stimulates cytochrome C oxidase → ATP
- Mitophagy via fasting selectively destroys damaged mitochondria
- Biomarkers: NAD+ levels, lactate/pyruvate ratio, CoQ10, body temp patterns
- AI transfer: Mitochondria = compute allocation per engine. ATP rate = tokens/second budget. Mitophagy = curator archival.

### Digestive / Microbiome
- 90% serotonin gut-produced. 70% immune cells in gut. Bidirectional vagus nerve communication.
- Optimization: eliminate inflammatory foods, probiotic diversity (fermented foods > pills), prebiotic fiber, 16:8 IF, digestive enzymes
- "Leaky gut" = gut permeability. Fix: L-glutamine, zinc carnosine, colostrum
- AI transfer: Microbiome = skill/plugin ecosystem diversity. Leaky gut = tools callable when shouldn't be. 90% serotonin gut = most resilience from periphery.

### Sleep / Recovery (Circadian Biology)
- Sleep consolidates memories (hippocampus→neocortex), clears waste (glymphatic), repairs (GH, T)
- Key: morning light (10K+ lux resets SCN), cool room (65F/18C), blue light elimination after sunset
- 90-min ultradian cycles (light→deep→REM). 5-6 cycles optimal.
- Caffeine: no coffee after 2pm (6hr half-life)
- AI transfer: the specialist engine = circadian engine. Glymphatic clearance = context compression. 90-min ultradian = focused work rhythm. Core temp drop = compute throttle.

### Immune System
- Chronic low-grade inflammation = root of modern disease. Not infection — dysregulation.
- Sauna 4-7x/week at 80C+ → 40% all-cause mortality reduction (Patrick). HSP70 = mechanism.
- Cold exposure: short-term suppression (Hof Radboud study) followed by rebound strengthening = hormesis.
- Fasting autophagy clears senescent cells triggering chronic inflammation.
- AI transfer: Immune = anomaly detection + critical slowing watchdog. HSP = refactoring cycles. Chronic inflammation = alert fatigue. Autoimmunity = agent-agent conflicts.

### Musculoskeletal
- Muscle = metabolic organ (largest glucose sink). Sarcopenia accelerates aging.
- BFR training: occlude limbs at 50-70% arterial pressure for hypertrophy at 20-30% loads.
- Occam's Protocol: 2 exercises, 5-7min each, 2x/week. Maximum stimulus, minimum time.
- AI transfer: Sarcopenia = capability atrophy. BFR = constrained execution. Protein synthesis = build cycle. Fascia = inter-engine handoff protocols.

### Detox / Liver
- Two-phase: Phase 1 (cytochrome P450 modifies toxins) → Phase 2 (conjugation for excretion). Block Phase 2 = accumulated modified toxins = MORE damage.
- Support: bitter herbs, cruciferous vegetables, glutathione, NAC, sauna sweating
- AI transfer: the specialist engine = liver quality gate. Phase 1/2 = generate then filter pipeline. Binders = memory pruning. Curator = detox organ.

### Sexual / Reproductive
- Downstream of entire endocrine cascade. Biomarker of overall health.
- Nitric oxide support (beetroot, citrulline, arginine) for vasodilation.
- AI transfer: Reproduction = agent spawning. Coupling matrix triples encode "genetics." Bus bandwidth = vasodilation.
