# TSC 2026 Company Page Repost Pattern

Pattern for writing a repost blurb from the Divinity Science company page
that reposts the principal's personal TSC 2026 announcement.

## Approved Structure

1. **Emotional opener** (2-3 words, genuine excitement: "We are beyond proud.")
2. **The news** with FULL PAPER TITLE in quotes and sole author credit
3. **What Divinity Science was built for** (positions the company, not just the person)
4. **Broader vision** (health, performance, medicine — not just AI architecture)
5. **Tease** what is coming WITHOUT overcommitting
6. **Closing line** (short, emotional)

## Approved Blurb (2026-08-03, final)

```
We are beyond proud.

Our founder the principal Williams just got accepted to present "Physiomorphic AI: Engineering Consciousness Correlates Through Biological Isomorphism" at The Science of Consciousness 2026 in San Diego. Sole author.

This is what Divinity Science was built for. Over 100 specialized engines structured to mirror a biological body, live right now, and now going in front of one of the most demanding scientific audiences in the world.

But Physiomorphic AI is only the beginning. The same architecture that maps a living body onto software is the foundation for everything we are building: health systems that think in systems the way the body actually works, performance science that models the whole person not just one metric, and research tools built for the complexity that biology actually demands.

The structural map points at medicine, at human performance, at the questions that matter most when things get hard. This is bigger than AI architecture. This is a new way to model life under pressure.

We cannot wait to show you what is coming.
```

## Corrections (3 rejections to final)

### Rejection 1: Too flat, not exciting enough

First draft was: "Our founder the principal Williams is presenting Physiomorphic AI at..."
the principal: "this doesn't reflect enough excitement like an announcement should"

Fix: Open with genuine emotion ("We are beyond proud."). Use "just got accepted" (event,
not status). Short punchy sentences.

### Rejection 2: Overpromised what will be shared

Second draft promised: "Over the coming weeks the principal is taking the full architecture
public for the first time. The structural mapping. The measurement framework."

the principal: "whoa... hold up... i will NOT put the full architecture live"

Fix: Remove ALL promises about what will be shared. Close with "We cannot wait to show
you what is coming" (vague tease, no specifics about architecture being made public).

### Rejection 3: Not enough about the bigger vision

Third draft was clean but the principal: "do a bit more about teasing what is to come and also
how this is more than just physiomorphic ai... this is all about health and all the other
things i always talk about."

Fix: Add two paragraphs connecting to Divinity Science's broader mission: health systems,
performance science, research tools, medicine, human performance. The repost must position
Physiomorphic AI as the FOUNDATION for everything DS is building, not the entirety of it.

## Hashtag Strategy (Company Repost)

Company repost uses a DIFFERENT hashtag set from the personal post. Zero overlap.
Must use HIGH-VOLUME VIRAL tags that actually trend on LinkedIn, NOT niche tags.

**Approved company repost hashtags (2026-08-03, viral set):**

```
#Health #Healthcare #Medicine #MentalHealth #Biotech #PublicHealth
#Pharma #MedicalDevices #AI #MachineLearning #Innovation
#Sustainability #Leadership #Growth #Community
```

**CORRECTION (2026-08-03, binding):** the principal rejected the niche hashtag set (#HealthTech #DigitalHealth #MedTech #Bioinformatics #SystemsBiology #ComputationalBiology etc.) as "not currently viral." These tags have low search volume on LinkedIn despite being topically accurate. The approved set above uses broad high-volume tags (#Health, #Medicine, #MentalHealth, #Biotech, #Leadership, #Community) that consistently trend. Same rule as personal post: use ALL viral tags, no niche tags.

The personal post uses: #AI #ArtificialIntelligence #Innovation #Tech #MachineLearning
#Neuroscience #Future #Science #Research #Startup #EmergingTech #AIArchitecture
#Biotech #Computing #TechCommunity

## Voice Rules

- Third-person company voice ("Our founder," "we," "Divinity Science")
- Genuine excitement, not corporate press release tone
- Must sound like a real person wrote it fast because they were excited
- No "reach out" (AI tell). No "genuinely" (AI tell).
- Full paper title in quotes (not shortened)
- No "poster" or "poster number" (same rule as personal post)
