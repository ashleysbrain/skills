## 3 FAILs Found

### FAIL 1: "Same indicators consciousness science has been developing for decades" is factually wrong for 2 of 4 items

Self-regulation (Sterling allostatics) and adaptive response (immune-layer learning) are NOT consciousness indicators. They come from physiology and immunology. Integration (IIT/Tononi) and differentiation (GWT/Baars/Dehaene) DO come from consciousness theory.

**Fix:** Separate by source. "Integration and differentiation come from consciousness theory. Self-regulation and adaptive response come from the rest of biology."

### FAIL 2: "Substrate to test against" is a consciousness proximity trap

In consciousness science, "substrate" means the physical substrate of conscious experience (PSC, NCC). Calling the operator agent a "substrate" implies it could bear conscious experience.

**Fix:** "the operator agent is a system where those properties can be measured together, on some thing you can actually instrument." Removes "substrate" entirely.

### FAIL 3: "Drug interactions" has zero pharmacological basis

Drug-drug interactions require pharmacological modeling at molecular/receptor level (CYP 450, receptor binding affinities). the operator agent has no pharmacological component. Quotable in isolation. A single hostile screengrab would undermine the paper.

**Fix:** Remove "Drug interactions" entirely. Replace "cascade failures in the body" with "cascade failures in complex systems." Add explicit disclaimer: "Not in patients, not yet, and maybe not ever."

## Medical Claims Safety Pattern

Every medical/clinical implication paragraph must follow this structure:

1. **Conditional opener:** "If that holds..."
2. **What the system COULD do:** "...could give researchers a new way to model what happens when coordination breaks down."
3. **Explicit scope limit:** "In software, not in patients."
4. **Specific examples WITHOUT pharmacology:** "Disease states. Cascade failures in complex systems."
5. **Explicit disclaimer:** "Not in patients, not yet, and maybe not ever."
6. **Forward-looking close:** "But the structural map is there, and biology is where medicine's hardest unsolved problems live."

Forbidden medical claims:
- "Drug interactions" (no pharmacological basis, ever)
- "Cascade failures in the body" (implies biological relevance; use "in complex systems")
- "Models how biological systems coordinate" (use "built on biological coordination principles")
