# principal LinkedIn Science Page

The principal's personal LinkedIn page, positioned as science, carrying the operator agent and
the OS as PROJECTS with IP-safe writings engineered for reach. The voice
law (`social-media/the operator agent-public-voice`) governs her voice; this skill governs
the principal's page architecture and the projects section.

## When to Use
- principal asks to revamp their personal LinkedIn "as science" or with the operator agent/Mosaic
  OS as projects
- Building the writings plan (posts, carousels, field notes) for the page
- Any order naming viral reach for the principal page

## Procedure

1. **Strategy artifact FIRST, copy second.** When the order names reach or
   virality, deliver a written strategy (page frame, viral axis, flywheel,
   cadence) before any paste-ready copy. A principal who asked for deep strategy
   should see the strategy before the copy it produced.
2. **Page frame (proven):** a scientist's lab notebook that contains two
   living projects. Science-first, never founder-hustle. Banner: gold
   butterfly on charcoal, one line ("I build organisms that learn").
3. **Viral axis = only-she-can-say.** The sentences no other account could
   write are the shareable unit. "My adversarial panel caught me overclaiming
   and I shipped the correction" is both the content and the proof.
4. **Flywheel:** comment lane (daily, proven) → author replies → replies
   become field notes → best notes become featured pins → recurring format
   builds follows. Cadence: 3-5 comments/day, 2 owned posts/week, 1 physics
   carousel/month.
5. **Projects section:** two entries, ~2,000 chars each, full paste-ready.
   Shape: category-planting opener → one concrete image ("an organism that
   keeps its own house") → principles doing real work → receipts (dates,
   counts, laws minted) → honest state label ("running in production for
   our own research; not a product"). the operator agent's entry centers the signature:
   "She signs everything she writes. The signature is the experiment."
6. **IP boundary:** the five banned categories in
   `writing-studio/gg_voice.py` apply to ALL page copy. Mechanisms and
   receipts ship; engine internals and unreleased specifics never.
7. **Self-claims audit before delivery:** grep every claim for artifact
   backing; state labels checked against the reality ladder
   (BUILT-RUNNING / INERT / DESIGNED / ORDERED-ONLY). An overstated entry
   is a public credibility event.
8. **Decision seams to the principal as buttons** when the frame has a real fork
   (scientist-forward vs founder-first vs organism-forward); recommended
   option first. On timeout, proceed on the recommended path and say so.

## Pitfalls
- **Do not add a 6th top-level element where the principal sized the surface to 5** —
   the projects live in the Projects section and the Home deck, entered
   through existing structures, never as new top-level surface.
- **Do not write founder-hustle framing under a science order** — momentum
   language ("building the future of") reads as category-competitor copy;
   the lab-notebook frame is what the order means by "so it's science".
- **Never invent principal interiority** in page copy — verified facts plus her
   own words; the page speaks as her, not as the organism.
