# Sequential-panel design + structural anti-rush findings (2026-08-25 volley session)

principal doctrine session on the QA/glial volley loop. The 10-brain sequential
panel produced findings that generalize to any adversarial-panel design.

## Sequential panel mechanics (principal requirement)

"10 different perspectives... they don't run in parallel" — brains run ONE
AT A TIME, each reads all predecessors' outputs, and each must ATTACK (find
what predecessors got wrong) then add exactly ONE new finding. The
sequential structure produces a chain of corrections, not a chorus of
agreement. Honesty header discloses: 1 process, N sequential roles, model ID.

## Structural findings that generalize

1. **Reward formulas invert their exploits, they don't remove them.**
   Inverting a priority formula (cheap-shallow over expensive-thorough)
   just flips the gaming to over-claiming depth. What resists gaming:
   DIMENSIONLESS ratios with MEASURED denominators (verified_info_gain /
   actual wall-clock cost), never agent-declared quantities.

2. **Queue pressure produces rushing structurally, not by choice.**
   Time-pressure literature: deadline pressure narrows attention and biases
   toward first satisficing answer. The fix is INPUT-side, not outcome-side:
   queue-opacity for executors (they see ONE task + rubric + own budget,
   never fleet dashboards or backlog counts). Rush is contagious through
   shared telemetry — displayed metrics become pace-setters (Goodhart +
   anchoring). Audience firewall: fleet-level numbers to the capacity
   reviewer only.

3. **Trigger capacity review on the DERIVATIVE (queue growth rate dL/dt),
   not a fixed threshold.** A threshold that fires at saturation arrives
   exactly when the system can no longer respond (critical slowing:
   relaxation time diverges near the phase transition).

4. **Agreement gates fail at the seams** — presence-checks instead of
   substance, function-gates instead of DB invariants, and correlated
   reviewer context = one brain agreeing with itself. A rounds-to-agreement
   metric REWARDS logrolling collusion; concession needs stake-carrying
   seats.

5. **Persistent registries rot three ways** — stale personas (model
   underneath swapped), dissent ossification (old objections re-litigated
   forever), single-point-of-failure memory. Seats need model-lineage,
   defense batting-average, demotion of zero-catch seats, and explicit
   dissent RESOLVED/WAIVED status.

6. **Hard size limits breed split-theater.** Rejecting est_cost > N outright
   causes agents to mint two N-0.1 subtasks sharing hidden state. Soft
   saturating penalty + auto-split OFFER preserves the law without creating
   the incentive to lie at the boundary.

7. **Every-loop-iteration-produces-something invariant:** each cycle ends
   with a minted task, a ledger entry, or an escalation — never silent.
