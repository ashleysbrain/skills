### Behavioral Science
- **Johns Hopkins habit formation study** (Nature Communications, June 3, 2026): Habits form as an abrupt phase transition, NOT a gradual slope. The transition from goal-directed to habitual behavior flips suddenly, like water freezing. Mouse study with advanced brain imaging. Challenges Lally et al. "66 days average" and the entire gradual-formation paradigm. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-71048-0.
- **Wendy Wood habit science profile** (APA Monitor, Jan 2026): General-audience piece on applying habit science, useful for framing.
- **Implication for DS**: Habit-tracking features based on streak-count or gradual progress bars may be built on an outdated model. Celebration/reinforcement timing (within 1-3 seconds) may be the threshold trigger, not raw repetition count.
