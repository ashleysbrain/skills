## Digital Health Regulation – FDA 2026
- **Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Guidance** updated Jan 6, 2026: enforcement discretion for software that meets CDS exemption but outputs a single clinically appropriate recommendation (provided other criteria met); previously required multiple options.
- **General Wellness Guidance** updated: expanded list of low‑risk general wellness products (e.g., certain sensor‑based wearables) that do not need FDA review if no medical claims.
- FDA struck language treating risk scores as devices, indicating a more permissive stance for low‑risk wellness AI.
- Overall: 2026 guidance shifts toward deregulation, enabling faster market entry for general‑wellness AI tools.

## Behavioral Science – Habit Formation Research (2026)
- **Johns Hopkins study** (Nature Communications, Jun 3, 2026): habit formation occurs far faster than previously believed; sudden neural “switch” from goal‑directed to automatic behavior.
- **Habit Loop**: cue → routine → reward; repetition strengthens neural pathways via long‑term potentiation.
- **Debunked 21‑day rule**: median 66 days for automaticity (Lally et al. 2010), range 18‑254 days.
- Requirements for sticky habit: consistent cue, felt craving, clear response, fast (immediate) reward.
- Implications: behavior change interventions must engineer cues, cravings, and immediate rewards to capture the fast‑forming automaticity switch.
