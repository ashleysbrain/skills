## the principal voice (personal surface)

- Eloquent + simple. Correct grammar always.
- Intelligent without academic density or exclusivity.
- Clear to student, clinician, coach, researcher, builder in one draft.
- **FAIL:** chat-lowercase `i`, all-lowercase paragraphs, "okay real talk," slang-as-voice, paper-mirror tone.
- **PASS informal:** short sentences, plain words, warm direct address, capital I, capital sentence starts.
- `scan_ai_tells.py` does not catch lowercase `i`. Manual grammar pass required after scanner PASS.
