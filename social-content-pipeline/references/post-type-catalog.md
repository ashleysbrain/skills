# Post Type Catalog for Social Content

Detailed examples and pairing guidance for each post type. Use when generating batches to ensure variety and coverage.

## 1. Curiosity Hook

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll with a surprising connection between two things people assume are unrelated.

**Hook pattern:** "X and Y are the same problem. You just can't see it yet."

**Best for:** Monday openers, first post of a new account, re-engagement after a gap.

**Length:** Short (150-275 chars). Let the hook breathe. Don't explain too much.

**Example:**
```
Your sleep and your bank account are the same problem. You just can't see it yet.

I'm building something that can.

Momentum Forecast. Breakthrough Windows. Leverage Points.

The system sees what you can't. Building in public starts now.
```

**Pairs well with:** System cascade image, network graph image.

## 2. Data Insight

**Purpose:** Credibility through real science or research findings.

**Hook pattern:** "[Study/source] found [surprising stat]."

**Best for:** Tuesday posts (analytical crowd), building trust with skeptical followers.

**Length:** Medium (250-290 chars). Name the study. Give the stat. Connect it to what you're building.

**Example:**
```
The INTERHEART study found that 90% of heart disease risk comes from modifiable behaviors.

Not genes. Behaviors. And they're connected.

That's why we track 8 dimensions as one coupled system, not 8 separate scores.

Behavioral physics is real. We're building the engine.
```

**Pairs well with:** Research infographic, coupling coefficient heatmap.

**Warning:** Don't fabricate statistics. Only cite real studies. If unsure, verify with web_search before posting.

## 3. Personal Story

**Purpose:** Vulnerability that proves the product works. "I learned the hard way" is the most powerful founder narrative.

**Hook pattern:** "I built X. It told me Y. I ignored it. [Bad outcome]."

**Best for:** Wednesday posts (memorable through the weekend), building emotional connection.

**Length:** Medium (250-280 chars). Short sentences. Fragments. The "I ignored it" moment is the hinge.

**Example:**
```
I built an AI that tracks 8 dimensions of my life. It told me my Tipping Point Alert was about to fire on sleep.

I ignored it.

Two weeks later, everything fell apart at once. Because it's one system.

I'm building it so you don't have to learn the hard way.
```

**Pairs well with:** System cascade flow, ripple diagram.

**Key:** The "I ignored it" admission is what makes this work. Nobody can copy founder vulnerability.

## 4. Category Break

**Purpose:** Define what you are by defining what you're NOT. Plants the category flag.

**Hook pattern:** "Most [competitors] do X. But [reality]. We do Y."

**Best for:** Early in account lifecycle, when people are trying to categorize you.

**Length:** Short-medium (200-260 chars). Three short paragraphs: the norm, the reality, the reframe.

**Example:**
```
Most apps pick one thing and go deep.

Sleep app. Budget app. Meditation app.

But your life isn't separate apps. Everything pulls on everything else.

We built a physics engine for human behavior. Not another app. A personal operating system.
```

**Pairs well with:** Split comparison image (bars vs nodes).

---

## 5. Founder Origin

**Purpose:** Why you built this for yourself first. Proves it's a real solution to a real problem, not a startup idea looking for a market.

**Hook pattern:** "I built the first version for myself."

**Best for:** Wednesday/Thursday, building founder credibility.

**Length:** Medium (250-300 chars). Describe the personal problem. The gap in the market. The decision to build.

**Example:**
```
I built the first version for myself.

My brain doesn't run default settings. I needed a system that understood a bad sleep night doesn't just mean being tired. It shifts everything: focus, spending, patience, motivation.

Nobody was looking at the connections.

So I built it.
```

**Pairs well with:** Network graph, ripple diagram.

**Key:** "My brain doesn't run default settings" is the user's approved replacement for clinical labels. Never name diagnoses directly.

## 6. Build Progress

**Purpose:** Building in public updates. Shows momentum, creates investment in the journey.

**Hook pattern:** "Week N of building in public: [accomplishment]."

**Best for:** Thursday posts, recurring weekly series.

**Length:** Short-medium (200-275 chars). Specific technical accomplishment. Not vague ("made progress"). Real details.

**Example:**
```
Week 1 of building in public:

Built the behavioral physics engine that maps 8 dimensions as a coupled system.

Not separate scores. Actual coupling coefficients. Sleep-Financial. Psychological-Physical. Real math, real connections.

More this week. Follow along.
```

**Pairs well with:** Coupling coefficient heatmap, system diagram.

---

## 7. Competitive Reframe

**Purpose:** Handle the inevitable "so you're like X?" question before it even gets asked.

**Hook pattern:** "So you're like [competitor]? No. [Competitor] does X. We do Y."

**Best for:** Any day, especially when you see comparisons in mentions/DMs.

**Length:** Short-medium (200-265 chars). Acknowledge the comparison. Reframe sharply. End with your category.

**Example:**
```
"So you're like Noom for your whole life?"

No. Noom optimizes habits. We optimize the system that produces them.

Your sleep tugs on your finances. Your mood tugs on your spending. That's not habits. That's physics.

We track the pull.
```

**Pairs well with:** Split comparison image.

---

## 8. Community Question

**Purpose:** Engagement. Gets replies. Builds community. The algorithm rewards replies.

**Hook pattern:** "What's the one [thing] that, when [it goes wrong], [everything else]?"

**Best for:** Friday posts (weekend engagement), end-of-week community building.

**Length:** Medium (240-270 chars). Share your own answer first. Then ask.

**Example:**
```
What's the one thing in your life that, when it goes wrong, everything else falls apart?

For me it's sleep. When sleep breaks, my spending drifts, my patience thins, my motivation evaporates.

That's not 3 separate problems. It's 1 system.

What's yours?
```

**Pairs well with:** No image needed (text-only posts often get more engagement).

---

## 9. Worldview

**Purpose:** Philosophy stated as product vision. Not a mission statement (those sound corporate). A belief stated so sharply it IS the product.

**Hook pattern:** "[Value statement]. That's not a [generic thing]. That's the product."

**Best for:** Any day as a palette cleanser. Not overused (1 per batch max).

**Length:** Short (150-210 chars). Three lines max.

**Example:**
```
Healthy people are kinder people. Kinder people build better worlds.

That's not a mission statement. That's the product.

We start with the individual. The ripple goes outward from there.
```

**Pairs well with:** Ripple diagram.

---

## Mixing Rules

- **Never post the same type two days in a row.**
- **Curiosity + Personal Story + Community Question = the core rotation.** These three drive the most engagement.
- **Data Insight posts need real sources.** If you can't verify the stat, use a different post type.
- **Build Progress is weekly only.** Don't post "updates" more than once per week or it feels like noise.
- **Worldview is monthly only.** Too frequent and it sounds preachy.
