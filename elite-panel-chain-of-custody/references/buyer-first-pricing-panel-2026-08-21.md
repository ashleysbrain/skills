# Buyer-First Pricing Panel (2026-08-21) — panel #4 rejection → panel #5 recovery

The session where a panel was run on the wrong question, rejected by the principal, and re-run correctly. Read before running any pricing or product-validation panel.

## The failure (panel #4, "Ask the Fleet" $49-brief validation)

Brief asked: "is $49 the right price?" Five seats (Kahneman, Christensen, Deming, Djilas, Kim) all priced the product. Only one (Christensen) attacked buyer existence. The buyer was assumed by the brief, never derived.

Two chair sins compounded it:
1. **Split-verdict averaging.** Christensen: KILL $49 (per-question pricing dead: Consensus answers cited health questions free, Deep Research ships in a ~$20/mo subscription, Undermind $16/mo unlimited, Examine ~$199/yr category ceiling). Kahneman: KEEP as impulse probe (below deliberation threshold). The chair's fold averaged them into "$49 as lead qualifier" — a hybrid NO seat argued for. Kahneman's entire premise (impulse buyer) presupposed the buyer Christensen had disproven.
2. **Averaged fold contradicted the principal's read.** principal rejection verbatim: "they don't understand my system why would they ask a random ai system a question for $50??? this doesn't equate... and the $2,500? for what? what all does that entail? you are doing this with too generalist of a brain..... the panel or output or soemthing was too narrow as I am definitely not confident in this answer."

## The recovery (panel #5, buyer-first re-derivation)

Brief reframed to ask IN ORDER: WHO has the problem → WHAT they already pay (live-verified anchors) → WHAT deliverable in what FORM → price LAST. Five seats (Rogers, Christensen, Deming, Djilas, Tetlock), sequential in one child, converged cleanly:

- **No consumer price point exists** (5 methods, one answer: information job served free; the job consumers would pay for — "what should I take" — is the one the content line forbids).
- **Pilot = primary lane** ($5.5-9.5K warm band, 65/35/0): the only buyer whose current spend already flows to the fleet's exact capability.
- **Pack = warm-only probe; the FIRST BUYER NAMES THE NUMBER.** Honest labor band $1.0-3.75K (Kolabtree $50-125/hr live, ZipRecruiter lit-review ~$48/hr, retail floor $8/page). $2.5K-by-fiat was priced by lineage, never by a buyer.
- Mode outcome stays $0; only asks-out + Stripe link + one timed rehearsal move probability.

## Laws extracted (now binding)

1. **Buyer EXISTENCE before price.** Never brief a panel on "is $X right" — brief it on "who has the problem and what do they pay."
2. **Split verdicts are UNRESOLVED, never averaged.** Decide on merit (name the principle) or escalate to the principal as an open fork.
3. **A verdict priced without a named-buyer conversation on record repeats the failure** (Tetlock's flag).
4. **Price-by-lineage is fabrication-adjacent:** "the pilot's day-1 deliverable" defined a $2.5K product whose contents had never been specced. Define the artifact before the price.

## Delegation patterns proven this session

- **Skip-if-exists re-dispatch:** after an owner-death, re-dispatch with "if <output>.md already exists with real content, skip that seat — a prior attempt may have landed." Prevents duplicate seat runs across crashed children.
- **Readback verification of child self-reports:** child said "3 files written" → chair read all three in full before consolidating. Readback catches the class where a child claims writes that never landed.
- **Brief must cite only verified files:** one brief cited a capability file that did not exist (phantom reference) — child burned API calls hunting it. Verify every path in the brief before dispatch.
- **Sibling-session detection:** before editing shared strategy docs, `stat` the tree for mtimes newer than your last read. This session found a sibling's sweep + map fold landed mid-turn (16:09-17:47 mtimes) with an opposing fold. Resolution: principal rulings stamp into shared artifacts as dated blockquotes and supersede any panel fold, sibling or chair.
- **Chair-correction protocol for seat files:** when a seat's "verified" claim is false (e.g., "billing files DO NOT EXIST" — they existed, unexecuted), append a dated CHAIR CORRECTION to the seat file itself, preserving the original text; never silently rewrite a verdict.
