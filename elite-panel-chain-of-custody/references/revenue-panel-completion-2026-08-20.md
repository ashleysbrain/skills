- **Two-phase final seat:** falsifier then consolidator in seat 9 — attack the converged plan BEFORE the principal reads it, then fold repairs into the consolidation as binding edits.
- **Kill-radius ranking:** rank a plan's assumptions by how many downstream conclusions die with each; the maximal-radius assumption with no decision-tree branch is the plan's true single point of failure (here: outbound capacity, not reply rates).
- **Convergence is load-bearing:** when two seats independently derive the same number (one panelist's economic floor matched another's floor), bank it as a load-bearing constant, not coincidence.
- **Fallback-into-unverified audit:** every fallback branch must name the asset it pivots to and check that asset is VERIFIED — a fallback that assumes its own premise is a hidden single point of failure.
- **Curator write-gate deadlock (tooling note):** in a review-only turn, `skill_manage` write/patch refuses writes to files whose content `skill_view` dedup-marked "unchanged" (content_returned: false) — the read-gate demands fresh content, the dedup guard refuses to return it.
