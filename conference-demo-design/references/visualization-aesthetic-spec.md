# Visualization Aesthetic Specification for Living-System Demos

## Source: Refik Anadol Studio methodology applied to poster constraints

## The Core Principle
Dead data is displayed. Living data is breathed. The visualization must feel ALIVE before anyone touches it.

## The Idle State (most critical moment)

The screen must show a living system in motion BEFORE interaction. This is what draws people from 10 feet away across a poster hall.

### What it looks like:
- Engine/system points scattered as a luminous constellation on night sky (#080d1a)
- NOT a grid. NOT a circle. NOT any regular geometric pattern. Organic, grown, emergent.
- Connected by filament lines at 8-12% opacity (felt before seen)
- The constellation BREATHES: 4.5s inhale (expansion), 5.5s exhale (contraction). Total 10s cycle.
- Points shift slightly during each breath, like stars shimmering through atmosphere
- Soft corona around brighter points (moonlight gold #e8d5a0), like candlelight (noisy, warm, imperfect)
- 40-60 particles drifting along filaments at varied speeds (8-40px/s)
- Background: Perlin/simplex noise oscillating between #080d1a and #0d1219, 20-30s cycles
- Film grain at 2-3% opacity to break digital smoothness
- Asymmetric composition: densest cluster offset to upper-left-third

### Motion specifics:
- Each point pulses at its own rate (1.2-3.5s per cycle), consistent per point
- Brightness oscillation: 30-70% of max
- No particle moves in a straight line
- Particle trails: 3-5 frames of decay (phosphor-like)

## Input Flow (when attendee enters their data)

### Phase 1 - Ingestion (0-2s)
Text dissolves into particles (not fade). Particles stream from input device to main display.

### Phase 2 - Routing (2-5s)
Particles enter constellation at the routing point. Split and flow along filaments. Filaments brighten from 8-12% to 40-60% opacity, warm shift toward gold. Engine points flare, coronas expand, pulse rate increases. Travel takes 2-3 seconds. Slowness = body, not machine.

### Phase 3 - Resonance (5-8s)
New connections emerge between engines, one by one (0.4s per line, 0.2-0.5s stagger). Each new connection sends a ripple of light. Breathing shifts: faster, more intense.

### Phase 4 - Fingerprint (8s+)
Constellation settles into unique configuration. Hidden connections pulse gently, each at its own rhythm. No two inputs produce the same pattern.

## Color Discipline (four colors only)

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Night sky | #080d1a | Background base |
| Moonlight gold | #e8d5a0 | Active states, coronas, input field border |
| Starlight | #c4d4e8 | Idle filaments, particle trails, dim points |
| Indigo | #6366f1 | SPARINGLY for hidden connections (2-3 instances max) |

Adding a fifth color is how you make it mediocre.

## The Input Device (iPad)

NOT a control panel. An ALTAR.
- Single luminous text field
- Placeholder: "What is consciousness?" (not "Enter your theory")
- The question is posed BY the system TO the researcher
- After submission: companion view of activated region (detail), not duplicate of main display

## Instant Death Mistakes

1. Grid layout or any regular geometric arrangement
2. Buttons, submit buttons, web form elements
3. Bright backgrounds (white, light gray)
4. Charts (bar, radar, pie, heatmap) - makes it look like measurement, not being
5. Uniform motion (all particles same speed = screensaver)
6. Always-on labels (turns constellation into diagram)
7. Off-palette colors
8. CSS box-shadow glow (must look like light through a medium, not digital glow)

## Why Breathing Matters

The deepest recognition cue the human nervous system has for "something is alive" is respiratory-rate motion. A still image is an object. A breathing image is a presence. This is physiological, not aesthetic. From 10 feet away, before anyone reads the poster title, they must see a light that is alive and feel pulled toward it.

The best art does not win arguments. It makes arguments irrelevant. For eleven seconds, the question "is this alive?" should stop being a question and start being an experience.
