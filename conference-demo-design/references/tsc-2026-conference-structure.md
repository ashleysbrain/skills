## Consciousness Mini-Hackathon (KEY OPPORTUNITY)

**What:** A room set aside throughout the conference for coding, tinkering,
and exchanging ideas. Self-organized side event, NOT a competition.

**Logistics:**
- Open throughout the conference
- Drop in between sessions, evenings, whenever
- Bring laptop, data, your own AI subscription (Claude Code, Codex mentioned)
- Facilitators present
- No registration required

**Stated activities:**
- Build something new (analysis pipeline, visualization, experimental paradigm)
- Collaborate on a paper (AI-assisted writing, idea to draft within conference)
- Exchange methods (show your workflow, learn theirs)
- Just hang out (work alongside others)

**Past hackathon outputs:** EEG smartphone app, philosophical argument mappers.

**Contact to be a moderator:** info@tsc2026.org

**Why this is the prime insertion point for a DS product:**
- Researchers are actively building and sharing
- The room is designed for method exchange and collaboration
- Small, focused audience (30-50 at a time) enables tight viral loops
- Each person who shares a method becomes discoverable to everyone else
- "Method Exchange" tool concept born from this research

## Tool Implications

The conference structure reveals these insertion points for a viral DS tool:

1. **Hackathon room** — researchers actively sharing methods. Prime for a
   "Method Exchange" tool (local-first, QR-based method card sharing).
2. **Poster sessions** — 200+ posters, universal pain. Prime for navigation tools.
3. **Concurrent tracks** — 4 parallel sessions, attendees must choose.
   Prime for schedule/conflict resolution tools.
4. **Plenary sessions** — high-profile speakers. Prime for real-time engagement.
5. **Conference-wide** — identity and networking. Prime for Camp Compass.
