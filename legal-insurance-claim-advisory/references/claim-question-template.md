# Claim Intake Question Template (38 Questions)

## POLICY & COVERAGE (Progressive) — 7 questions
1. What is the Progressive policy number?
2. What coverages are active? (Collision, BI, PD, MedPay, UM/UIM, Rental, Roadside)
3. What are the policy limits for each coverage?
4. Does the policy have any ADAS / autonomous / driver-assist exclusions? (Most do NOT, but verify)
5. Is the vehicle leased, financed, or owned outright?
6. Is there gap insurance?
7. When was the policy effective?

## THE ACCIDENT — 9 questions
8. Date, time, and exact location? (City, state, road, intersection)
9. Was Autopilot or FSD engaged at impact? (CENTRAL question)
10. Did the Tesla give warnings before the crash? (Lane departure, forward collision, take-over alert)
11. Weather, road, and traffic conditions?
12. Other vehicles involved? How many? Who had right of way?
13. Police called? Report number and jurisdiction?
14. Witnesses? Names and contact info?
15. Traffic cameras or nearby business surveillance?
16. What was driver doing before impact? (Hands on wheel? Eyes on road?)

## THE VEHICLE & TESLA DATA — 7 questions
17. Tesla model, year, and trim?
18. Autopilot (basic) or FSD (beta/supervised)?
19. Software version at time of accident?
20. Has the vehicle been repaired? If not, where is it?
21. Was the vehicle totaled or repairable?
22. Has a Tesla preservation letter been sent? (MOST URGENT — Tesla overwrites data in 30-90 days)
23. Did Tesla reach out proactively?

## INJURIES & TREATMENT — 8 questions
24. What specific injuries? (Body parts, diagnoses)
25. Emergency treatment? (ER, ambulance, urgent care)
26. Current treatment plan? (Specialists, PT, imaging, pain management)
27. Still treating? What is next? (Surgery? Injections? Long-term therapy?)
28. Pre-existing conditions in same body areas?
29. Work missed? How many days? Income lost?
30. Impact on principal / the specialist engine work? (Panels, writing, conferences — economic damages)
31. Impact on sleep, daily activities, quality of life? (Start pain journal NOW)

## PROGRESSIVE ACTIONS TO DATE — 7 questions
32. Has Progressive accepted the claim? Claim number?
33. Has Progressive sent an adjuster? What did they say?
34. Has Progressive made ANY settlement offer? Amount?
35. Has Progressive requested a recorded statement? (DO NOT GIVE WITHOUT COUNSEL)
36. Has Progressive sent medical authorization forms? (DO NOT SIGN WITHOUT REVIEW)
37. Has Progressive mentioned subrogation against Tesla? (Get it in writing)
38. Has Progressive sent a vehicle damage estimate?

## IMMEDIATE ACTION ITEMS (Priority Order)
1. **TESLA PRESERVATION LETTER** — certified mail + email to Tesla Legal (EDR, Autopilot logs, sensor data, cabin camera, disengagement events, OTA history)
2. Request complete Progressive policy documents
3. Start injury documentation: daily pain journal, photos, ALL medical records
4. DO NOT give Progressive recorded statement without attorney
5. DO NOT sign Progressive medical authorizations without review
6. Retain local PI counsel in accident state (auto defect / Tesla experience)
7. Calculate special damages: medical bills, lost wages, property damage, rental
8. File NHTSA complaint at safercar.gov
