## FR029 Form Structure (Consumer Version)

### Section 1 — Information About You (OPTIONAL — leave blank for anonymous)
- First name, last name
- Street address, city, state, ZIP
- Phone number, email
- Today's date

### Section 2 — Information About the Suspect(s)
- First / middle / last name
- Street address, city, state, ZIP
- Phone number, email
- Occupation, employer
- Gender (Male/Female), Alias (AKA)
- Second suspect block (if applicable)

### Section 3 — Information About the Suspected Fraud
- When did the fraud happen? (date/timeframe)
- Brief summary of what happened
- List of witnesses
- Attach supporting documents/evidence

## Reporter Protections (stated on form)
- Cannot be sued for libel or slander for reporting suspected fraud
- Not required to give name or identifying information
- All WC fraud investigations kept confidential
- Filing meets state law requirement to report suspected fraud to TDI

## Statement Drafting Notes
- Form narrative field typically accepts ~1000 characters
- Use legal terms: "material misrepresentation," "collecting benefits under false pretenses," "violating medical restrictions"
- Reference evidence types explicitly: "surveillance video," "text messages," "photographs"
- If evidence is held by a third party, include their contact info in the statement
- Always verify phone numbers transcribed from audio — speech stutter produces invalid digit counts
