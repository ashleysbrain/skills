## Biological Isomorphism

A consortium is a **microbial consortium** — different specialist agents in
symbiotic metabolism of one substrate (the idea). Forms per-task, disbands on
completion. Different from Consilium (permanent operator brain-suite).

Lifecycle: ephemeral. Forms on idea intake, disbands on output completion.

```

HARD GATES (cannot mutate):
```

ALL 8 dimensions protected: Physical, Psychological, Intellectual, Social,
Spiritual, Vocational, Financial, Environmental. No hierarchy of harm.

The 8D gate measures HUMAN consumer wellness — what the marketing content
DOES TO THE HUMAN who sees it. NOT whether it is healthy for the AI to produce.
