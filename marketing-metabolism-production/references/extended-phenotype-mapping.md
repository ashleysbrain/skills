# Extended Phenotype Mapping Methodology

## The Problem

The `body-part-tackle-workflow` hard rule says "Exactly 11 Gray's Anatomy
systems. NO hybrids." But the organism has functions that extend BEYOND
standard anatomy — marketing, clothes/posture, clinical diagnostics, external
tools. These are **extended phenotype** structures (Dawkins, 1982): things
the organism builds or expresses that are part of its biological identity
but are not internal organs.

## Extended Phenotype Analog Patterns

Four patterns from biology for mapping non-standard organism functions:

### 1. Spider Web (Content Ecosystem)
- Web is built from the organism's own body, captures prey passively
- Physics: Hooke's law spring constants, structural optimization
- Marketing: content library captures attention passively (SEO, evergreen)

### 2. Birdsong (Content Distribution)
- Species-specific patterns, carries across distance, attracts/repels
- Physics: acoustic wave propagation with frequency-dependent attenuation
- Marketing: content distribution across platforms

### 3. Peacock Tail (Brand Display)
- Metabolically expensive honest signal (handicap principle, Zahavi)
- Physics: diminishing returns on investment, predation risk tradeoff
- Marketing: brand identity as costly honest signal (one-second test)

### 4. Pheromone Trail (Algorithmic Signaling)
- Laid by ants, reinforced by use, decays over time
- Physics: reaction-diffusion (Turing patterns)
- Marketing: SEO keywords, hashtags as pheromone trails

## Critical Pitfall: One Organ With Modes vs Multiple Organs

User correction (2026-06-29): Building 5 separate platform distribution engines is biologically wrong. The body has ONE exocrine gland that secretes to multiple surfaces through branching ducts. One organ, multiple outputs, regional specialization.

BUT: the control (ENS routing) and the secretion (exocrine gland) are DIFFERENT tissue types. Do NOT conflate them. The ENS is nervous tissue; the gland is epithelial tissue. Split when tissue types differ; use modes when tissue types are the same.
