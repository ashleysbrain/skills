## Op #8: Market/pricing claims
Any price, rate, or funnel percentage that steers a money decision gets graded against LIVE primary sources extracted THIS session — never memory, never panel consensus, never citations inside internal docs.

**Failure classes caught:**
- (a) **Circular provenance** — "calibrated" prices where dossier cites panels and panels cite the dossier. No external fact anywhere in the loop.
- (b) **Labor-hours pricing** — "25 expert-hours ≈ $2,500" prices the seller's cost. The demonstrated market for equivalent work (Kolabtree completed secondary-research projects, live page): $500-960/report. The list price was 2-3x demonstrated transactions with no named buyer.
- (c) **Stale reference classes** — funnel probabilities citing 2015-24 benchmarks against the org's 2025+ source rule.

**Evidence quality ladder:** completed transactions > advertised tiers > rate cards > panel estimates. Extract FULL pages (search snippets are not sources); cache them.

**PURGE RULE (extends "downgrade is not purge"):** when a price is downgraded to C, grep the ACTIVE plan/map/instrument files for the dead number and patch every live line. Historical seat/panel files stay untouched as records — but a dead price surviving in a live map re-infects the next fold. (This session: grep found 3 surviving $2.5K lines in the live map after the downgrade; all patched.)

**Split-verdict rule (extends "delegate re-injection" screening):** panel verdicts that split (kill + keep) are UNRESOLVED — escalate or test buyer existence. Never average them into a compromise product.
