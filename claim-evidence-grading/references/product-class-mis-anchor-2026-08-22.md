# Product-Class Mis-Anchor — Op #8 failure class (d) (2026-08-22, catch #4)

*Fourth principal rejection in the same campaign, ~1 hour after the labor-hours fix. Companion to market-pricing-claims-2026-08-22.md (classes a-c); extends it with class (d).*

## The catch
The Evidence Pack was re-anchored on Kolabtree's demonstrated $500-960/report — the textbook fix for labor-hours pricing. principal rejected it again: "I don't understand who would pay $500 for a generated response from unproven ai."

## Root cause
Kolabtree's price buys a **PhD human's accountable month** — the accountability IS the product. We sell generation. Re-anchoring a class-2 product on class-1 prices re-commits failure class (b) one level down: the number changed, the wrong evidence class didn't. Softening a dead price (list → "buyer-named band" around the same anchor) is defending the lane, not fixing it.

## The three price classes (never cross-anchor)
| Class | Buyer pays for | 2026 demonstrated prices |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Human-accountable service | A person staking reputation on the work | Kolabtree $500-960/report, $55-125/hr |
| 2. AI generation as product | Output, no accountability | Elicit/SciSpace $12-49/mo SUBSCRIPTIONS over 138-280M papers; per-report AI tops at $299-499 (VantaInsights 48-hr custom) |
| 3. AI output inside custom delivery | A built system + integration + support | Small-biz first AI project $3-10K (taskip/SuperDupr/Latenode converge) |

## Screening questions (ask BEFORE grading any price)
1. Which class is this product in? (Name it explicitly.)
2. Is the anchor from that class? (If a class-2 product cites class-1 numbers → automatic grade C.)
3. Is a subscription commodity adjacent to the SKU? (Caps standalone price at ~10-40x its monthly price; above that only accountability or custom delivery carries the number.)

## Verdict it forced (worked example)
Standalone AI evidence reports have no demonstrated buyer above $299-499 sitting next to $12/mo subscriptions → the Evidence Pack SKU died entirely; evidence work survives only (a) INSIDE the pilot as day-1 deliverable (class 3), (b) as free Letters (marketing). Ledger: revenue-panel/MARKET-EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md "AI-native comparables" section.

## Transferable rules
- "Who would pay this, and what EXACTLY are they paying for?" must answer in one sentence naming the class — if the sentence mixes "AI" with a human-service price, the anchor is wrong.
- When the principal rejects a number twice, the second re-derivation must change evidence/product class, not just the dollar figure.
