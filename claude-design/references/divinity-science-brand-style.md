# Divinity Science — LinkedIn & Social Media Brand Style Guide

Extracted from existing LinkedIn post graphics on 2026-06-09.

## Overall Visual Style

- **Dark mode aesthetic** — deep near-black background, rich dark navy/charcoal
- **Radial gradient lighting** — subtle center glow for depth, accent color varies per post
- **Atmospheric and futuristic** — premium tech-meets-mystical SaaS look

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #0D0F1A | Deep dark navy-black |
| Primary Text | #FFFFFF | Pure white for headlines |
| Secondary Text | #A0A4B8 | Muted silver-gray for body |
| Accent: Teal | #00D4AA | Bright cyan-teal — highlights, icons, lines |
| Accent: Gold | #F5A623 | Warm amber — alternate posts |
| Accent: Purple | #7B61FF | Electric purple — alternate posts, glow effects |
| Gradient glow | 12-25% opacity | Soft radial of accent color |

## Layout (LinkedIn Mobile — 1080×1350)

Three-zone vertical structure:
1. **Top**: "Divinity Science" wordmark + diamond icon
2. **Center**: Bold white headline (large sans-serif), sometimes with accent-colored stat callout
3. **Bottom**: Supporting gray text, bullet points, or quoted highlights

## Typography

- **Font family**: Inter, Poppins, or Segoe UI (modern geometric sans-serif)
- **Headlines**: 58-72px, bold/semibold, pure white
- **Body**: 22-28px, regular weight, muted gray (#A0A4B8)
- **Brand wordmark**: 18px, uppercase, letter-spacing 2px, "DIVINITY" white + "SCIENCE" accent color
- **Stats/numbers**: Large, accent-colored

## Recurring Brand Elements

- **Diamond/rhombus icon** — 28px, 2px border in accent color, rotated 45deg, with 8px inner square
- **Thin accent lines** — 1-3px horizontal dividers in accent color
- **Soft glow orbs** — blurred circular gradients at 12-25% opacity behind content
- **Dot grid overlay** — 1px dots at 40px spacing, 3% white opacity
- **Geometric shapes** — rounded rectangles, circles at 8-15% opacity as decorative elements
- **Post number watermark** — large (120px), ultra-low opacity (6%) in bottom-right, e.g. "01"
- **Bottom accent bar** — 4px gradient line (transparent → accent → transparent)

## Social Media Dimensions

- LinkedIn mobile-optimized: **1080×1350** (4:5 portrait)
- LinkedIn tall portrait: **1080×2340** (~9:19.5)
- All graphics use PNG for sharp geometric edges

## Image Generation Notes

- **No stock photos** — entirely abstract/geometric illustrative style
- **No photography** — use shapes, gradients, dot patterns
- **Single accent color per graphic** — rotate teal → purple → gold across a series
- **Content is minimal** — one big idea per graphic, lots of negative space
- **Series consistency** — same brand elements in same positions, only accent color and content change

## Style Variant: Warm Cream (Light Mode)

Extracted from earlier LinkedIn post series (Physics Engine campaign, 2026-06-09). Use when the content calls for a warmer, more approachable tone.

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #FAF7F2 | Warm cream/off-white |
| Primary Text | #2D2D2D | Dark charcoal |
| Secondary Text | #666666 | Medium gray for body |
| Accent: Terracotta | #D4836B | Warm earth-tone highlights |
| Accent: Sage | #8FAE8B | Muted green |
| Accent: Teal | #6B9C9E | Dusty teal |
| Accent: Gold | #C9A96E | Warm gold |

### Warm Cream Layout Notes

- **9:16 portrait** (1080×1920) — taller than dark mode posts
- **ALL CAPS sans-serif headers** — bold, uppercase, wide letter-spacing
- **Generous white space** — more breathing room than dark mode
- **Muted earth-tone geometric shapes** — wheels, arcs, cards at low opacity
- **No gradients or neon** — flat colors with subtle opacity variation only
- **Card-based layout** — information in rounded-corner cards on cream background
