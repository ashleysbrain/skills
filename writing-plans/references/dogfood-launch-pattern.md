# Dogfood launch pattern for roadmap and pitch-pack work

Use this pattern whenever a launch document, roadmap, or whiteboard needs to reflect internal adoption, proof generation, and marketing credibility.

## Core rule
The team should use the product and methodology internally before asking the market to trust it.

## What to say in the doc
- Internal use first
- Internal proof second
- Public story third
- Dogfood outputs become screenshots, bug reports, copy improvements, proof stories, and launch assets
- If it is confusing internally, it is not ready for users
- If the team will not use it, it should not ship

## What dogfood should generate
- screenshots from real sessions
- before/after stories
- bug reports and friction notes
- onboarding fixes
- copy improvements
- proof artifacts for landing pages, emails, and partner outreach

## How it fits the roadmap
- Put dogfood near the core product loop, not as an afterthought
- Tie dogfood to marketing so proof creation and channel content come from the same internal practice
- Use dogfood to sharpen the wedge, the promise, and the onboarding narrative

## Visual guidance
- Keep dogfood visible in the whiteboard / roadmap visual if space allows
- If space is tight, retain the dogfood rule as a banner or callout rather than burying it
- Prefer clear structure over decorative complexity
