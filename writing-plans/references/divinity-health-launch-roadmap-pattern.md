# Divinity Health Launch Roadmap Pattern

Session-derived notes for launching an open-core health product under a parent science brand.

## Brand architecture
- **Divinity Science** owns the methodology, standards, credibility, and educational narrative.
- **Divinity Health** owns the product, UX, subscriptions, and monetization.
- Keep the boundary explicit in the roadmap so the launch story and the revenue story stay aligned.

## Launch shape
- Do **not** launch as a generic app, chatbot, tracker, or meditation product.
- Start with **one wedge audience** and **one daily loop**.
- Optimize for proof before polish: usefulness, repeat return, and clear next action.
- Make the first product small enough to ship, understand, and refine quickly.

## Open-core boundary
- Open the community standard: baseline schema, scoring concepts, simple bot scaffolding, consent principles, and release scaffolding.
- Keep proprietary: predictive intelligence, hosted personalization, premium assets, enterprise dashboards, and API access.
- Phrase the moat as "open the method, monetize the intelligence."

## Monetization ladder
1. Free standard / community access
2. Consumer premium
3. Hosted API / platform
4. Enterprise
5. Services / implementation support

## Recommended sequencing
1. Positioning lock
2. Minimum lovable product
3. Small beta proof
4. Public launch preparation
5. Open-core release
6. Brand growth and proof assets
7. Premium conversion
8. Enterprise expansion

## Deliverable shape
- Prefer one board-ready master brief plus, when needed, one technical appendix.
- Keep the roadmap step-by-step, practical, and legible for non-experts.
- Separate the teaching layer from the business layer so the launch plan remains understandable and monetizable.
