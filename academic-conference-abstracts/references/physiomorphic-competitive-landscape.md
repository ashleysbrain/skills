## The Defining Distinction

**Neuromorphic mimics the neuron. Physiomorphic mimics the organism.**

The term exists in three other domains, none of which claim the AI architecture
paradigm the principal is defining.

## Domain 2: Biomedical Engineering / Neuromodulation (CLOSEST PRIOR ART)

### The NPBH Workshop Paper (primary source)

**Hwang, Falcone, Monaco, et al. (March 2026).** "The role of neuromorphic
principles in the future of biomedicine and healthcare." arXiv:2603.27716.
56 pages. Workshop report from the Neuromorphic Principles in Biomedicine and
Healthcare (NPBH) Workshop, October 2024.
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.27716

Uses "physiomorphic" 6 times, always in quotes. Attribution: **Tim Denison**
(Professor of Engineering Science, University of Oxford; formerly Medtronic).
Key passages:

1. p.365: "not just neuromorphic but also closed-loop 'physiomorphic' approaches
   that use physiological markers to adjust stimulation in real time"
2. p.372: "these physiomorphic approaches are inspired by human physiology"
3. p.1070: "creating a truly 'physiomorphic' system" (predictive + reactive)
4. p.1361: "Francisco Valero-Cuevas argued that neuromorphic/physiomorphic computing
   excels at embedded logic... because it can leverage insights from the physical
   substrate where learning occurs"

Denison uses it for: next-gen adaptive deep brain stimulation (DBS) devices that
go beyond neuron-mimicry to model whole-system physiology (homeostatic, circadian,
predictive). Hardware-focused, medical device context.

## The Gap Physiomorphic AI Fills

Nobody has:
1. Named the paradigm "Physiomorphic AI" as a defined computing field
2. Defined it as organism-mimicry (vs neuron-mimicry) in software architecture
3. Mapped biological organ systems onto computing architecture as a formal discipline
4. Combined it with physics-informed principles (superposition, entanglement, free
   energy, critical slowing, adiabatic)

The Denison/NPBH community uses "physiomorphic" as an adjective for medical hardware.
the principal uses it as a named paradigm for software architecture. Same word, different
concept, different domain, different IP class.
