## Connectionism (what it actually means)

Connectionism (Rumelhart & McClelland, PDP tradition) requires:
- Networks of simple processing units
- Distributed / subsymbolic representations
- Learning via weight adjustment (backprop, Hebbian)
- Parallel distributed processing

Modern deep learning is the lineal descendant.

## What multi-agent biological isomorphism is NOT

Physiomorphic AI (the operator agent) as implemented:
- Structured multi-agent system with anatomical mapping
- Dynamical systems coordination (Kuramoto, FEP, critical slowing)
- Structural correspondence metric I(S)
- Heritable coupling matrix (not learned weights)

This is orthogonal to connectionism. You can implement it with zero neural
network components.
