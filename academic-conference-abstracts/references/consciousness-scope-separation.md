## Required abstract sentence (when reporting resilience/isomorphism data)

> These resilience results support the operational relevance of biological isomorphism. They do not constitute measurements of consciousness correlates, which remain a separate measurement program.

Without this sentence, hostile reviewers will read "resilience data next to consciousness correlates" as overselling consciousness.

## Operational-definition language (must-fix pattern)

When describing correlate *instruments* that have not been run as reported data:

- Use **defined as / defined by** (or "operationalized as" if space allows)
- Do **not** use **measured as / measured by** -- that implies completed measurements

When reporting architecture stats that *were* computed:

- "Preliminary scoring ... yields I(S) = 0.80"
- "Preliminary monte carlo simulation finds ... approximately 0.27"

Keep maturity-framework definitions and resilience-measurement results in
**separate paragraphs**.
