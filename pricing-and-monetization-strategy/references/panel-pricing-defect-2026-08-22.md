# Panel-Minted Price Defect (2026-08-22 catch)

## The catch
principal rejected a revenue plan twice in one day. Verbatim signals:
1. "$50 for a question... they don't understand my system why would they ask a random ai system a question for $50??? this doesn't equate... and the $2,500? for what? ... you are doing this with too generalist of a brain"
2. "I don't know how I will find customers willing to pay $1500 with no proof of concept... that is still not logical in my mind"
3. "I don't trust this is getting done through the right process flows and research processes. this is sloppy work with the market and pricing. this is not quality"

## Root cause
Every dollar figure traced to LLM advisory panels (5 seats x 5 panels), formatted with calibration language ("CALIBRATED", confidence bands, reference classes). Three failure classes:

1. **Circular provenance** — the dossier cited panels; the panels cited the dossier. Zero external market facts in the chain. The dossier's own appendix admitted: "rates, comparables — ASSUMPTIONS."
2. **Labor-hours pricing** — "$2,500 ≈ 20-25 expert-hours" prices the seller's cost. The demonstrated market for equivalent work (Kolabtree COMPLETED secondary-research projects) was $500-960/report, ~1 month turnaround. The list price was 2-3x demonstrated transactions with no named buyer.
3. **Stale reference classes** — funnel probabilities cited 2015-24 cold-outreach benchmarks against the org's 2025+ source rule.

Also: split panel verdicts had been averaged into a "hybrid" product (kill + keep = qualifier) — when verdicts split, the result is UNRESOLVED, never averaged.

## The fix (worked, same session)
1. Six batched web searches + five FULL-page extractions (search snippets are not sources). Cached under <agent-home>/cache/web/.
2. Every price/rate/percentage graded A/B/C per claim-evidence-grading, each grade naming its source. Output = MARKET-EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md, declared the evidence of record; panel files inherit its grades.
3. Grade-C prices modeled to $0; dead numbers purged from ACTIVE files by grep (historical seat files stay as records).
4. Buyer-existence before price: no-list-price instruments where the first buyer names the number inside the demonstrated band.
5. No-proof protocol adopted from market sources (Client Ascension Jan 2026; Common Paper design-partner guide; SuperDupr 2026): concrete deliverable stack + risk reversal as closer + $500-1,000 micro-pilot as the small paid first step.

## Transferable rules
- A panel consensus is a hypothesis with good formatting. Market facts come only from extracted primary sources.
- "Verified" means a live page this session, not a citation inside an internal doc.
- Completed transactions > advertised tiers > rate cards > panel estimates (descending evidence quality).
- When the principal rejects a number twice, stop re-deriving it from the same class of source; change evidence class entirely.
- Buyer existence is proven by a named buyer saying a number, nothing else.
