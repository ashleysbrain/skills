# DS IP Protection: 4-Layer Disclosure Framework

Condensed from the June 2026 marketing/branding session. This framework governs what DS creative and marketing content is safe to publish and what must stay proprietary. It is the creative team's blood-brain barrier.

---

## Governing Principle

**Market the output, not the engine. Market the insight, not the formula. Market the result, not the mechanism.**

The organism is the most compelling brand story DS has. But marketing it creates a disclosure risk. Resolution: show the coupled-systems CONCEPT everywhere, but never reveal the specific coefficient VALUES, the engine implementations, or the organism internals.

The paradox that resolves the tension: the more DS shows its OUTPUT (visualizations, insights, results), the more impressive it appears. The more a competitor copies the output, the more they realize they need the engine underneath. The output is the best marketing for the engine, and the engine is never shown.

## The 4 Disclosure Layers

| Layer | What Goes Here | Where It Appears | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. PUBLIC** | The brand itself: 8-dimension framework, coupling CONCEPT (not values), cascade concept, organism visualization, philosophy, founder story, "the body is the supreme technology" | All public content: TikTok, IG, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, website | "We don't build apps. We grow organisms." |
| **2. SURFACE METHODOLOGY** | The coupled-systems modeling APPROACH described at concept level. Framework structure, not parameter values. | Academic papers, investor decks, technical blog posts | "We model health as a coupled dynamical system using a TWC formula" (without the specific weights) |
| **3. PROPRIETARY** | Trade secrets. NEVER published. | Internal only. Not in any external artifact. | Specific coupling coefficients (k=0.54), sigmoid parameters, biological priors, weight calibration, scoring pipeline code, corpus contents |
| **4. EXISTENTIAL** | Never discussed, even internally with non-core team. | Nowhere. | Brain-analog engine implementations, user health data (raw), competitive intelligence methodology |

## Pre-Publication IP Check (Mandatory Step 7b in the Creative Process)

Before any creative artifact leaves the creative faculty, run this check:

1. **Does the artifact contain specific numeric coefficient values?** If yes, replace with qualitative language ("strong coupling" not "k=0.54") or move to Layer 3 (internal only).
2. **Does the artifact name specific engines, port numbers, or ECS codes?** If yes, strip them. The public sees "the organism," not "the specialist engine on port 8120."
3. **Does the artifact reveal the BRAND-DNA.md file contents (the genome)?** If yes, strip. The brand HAS DNA; the DNA file does not leave the building.
4. **Does the artifact reveal the scoring pipeline or corpus structure?** If yes, strip. Publish the METHOD (coupled-systems modeling), not the IMPLEMENTATION.
5. **For academic papers specifically:** Publish the FRAMEWORK, not the PARAMETERS. Like pharma publishing clinical trial results without revealing drug synthesis. The TWC formula STRUCTURE is publishable; the calibrated weight VALUES are not.
