# Benchmark Positioning History & Open Question (2026-08-13)

## Status: FLAGSHIP POSITIONING IS OPEN

The drift-index positioning was built through a full physics validation cycle (6+1 dimensions,
7 frameworks, Bloch composite, 5 detectors) and then REJECTED by the principal at the strategy layer:

> "I don't like that being the benchmark. Maybe it's a part of it, but that is not,
> I don't think, going to be strong enough of a position in the market for us of a
> benchmark. So, go back and re-strategize and rethink about what the actual benchmark
> should be based on what the market need is, which is going to require market research."

## What survived the rejection

ALL technical assets remain valid and are now COMPONENTS looking for the right flagship:
- 6+1 AI health dimensions with corrected physics (see main SKILL.md)
- 7 advanced frameworks (Fisher, StochThermo, RG, Bloch, Spectral, EMA, Ring)
- Bloch sphere composite H=(theta, phi, r, t_c)
- 5-detector drift pipeline + 600-task suite design
- Hallucination chain-rule decomposition (most publishable piece per the specialist engine)

## What died

- "AI Drift Index" / "Behavioral Stability Score" as the headline product
- The implicit assumption that "nobody does public longitudinal drift tracking" alone
  justifies a flagship. Technical white space ≠ market demand.

## Root cause of the failed positioning

The first market analysis (Andreasen) was training-data synthesis, zero live sources.
It found the white space that fit our assets instead of finding where demand actually is.
Middle-out problem solving was skipped: we went physics-first, market-never.

## Correct sequence (dispatched post-rejection)

1. the specialist engine: LIVE web research on eval market (adoption, funding, press, viral
   mechanics, OpenAI/Anthropic/Google eval moves, METR/Epoch/Scale plays). Deliverable:
   market map + 3 positioning opportunities rated on market size, competition density,
   viral mechanics, defensibility, fit with physics assets.
2. the specialist engine: LIVE research on practitioner pain (what breaks in production evals miss,
   agent reliability gaps, enterprise trust requirements). Framed diagnostically:
   symptoms -> condition -> missing diagnostic test.
3. Cross both against physics assets to find where our advantage actually lands.
4. Drift engine becomes a component, not the flagship.

## Lessons

- Live citations required for strategy decisions. Training recall is not market research.
- A benchmark needs a market-pull story (who adopts it, why now, what pain) BEFORE a
  physics story. Harvey started from real legal work, then built the bench.
- Viral formula from the research that DID hold up: simple score + felt pain +
  headline-friendly + living leaderboard. The drift engine serves all four — as a
  component of whatever flagship the market research selects.
