# Health Insurance Business Review Notes

## Session takeaway
The strongest near-term launch wedge for the AI-native health insurance idea is *software-first employer benefits*, especially SMB / ICHRA / level-funded distribution.

## Why
- Employer health costs remain elevated and are still rising.
- High-cost claims are a persistent pressure point.
- AI is already in payer workflows, but there is rising scrutiny around prior authorization and claims review.
- ICHRA / flexible benefits models are a real distribution lane.

## Peptides and emerging care
- Peptides / GLP-1 support are commercially relevant.
- They should be treated as an adjacent partner and access lane, not the launch core.
- Red light / photobiomodulation, regenerative medicine, longevity clinics, and wearable-driven recovery are future-strategy lanes, not launch assumptions.

## Business questions the review should answer
- What is the first customer segment?
- What is the regulated core versus the adjacent bridge layer?
- How does the company lower friction and cost without creating claims/compliance risk?
- What competition is already proving the model?
- What should remain future-state only?

## Delivery rule
When asked to review the business side of the concept, do not stop at the panel plan. Produce a direct, business-facing conclusion about launch wedge, pricing pressure, compliance risk, and adjacent care lanes.
