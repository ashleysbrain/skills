# Session Notes: Health Insurance Panel

## What the user corrected
- The panel deliverable must be *personas first*, then the game plan.
- Each persona must have a full personality profile, not just a role label.
- The panel must be tailored specifically to the health-insurance company spin-off, not a generic insurer panel.
- The workflow must continuously mirror upstream talent gate protocols before every run.

## Latest ecosystem requirements to keep in scope
- telehealth for mental health
- telehealth for physical health
- behavioral health / therapy platforms
- nutrition coaching and meal-support ecosystems
- prescriptions / pharmacy access
- dental partners and benefit design
- a unified member experience that feels like one source / one front door even if the back end is hybrid

## Design nuance
- The user wants the insurance experience to feel unified across medical, mental health, nutrition, prescriptions, and dental.
- This does not require a literal single carrier on day one.
- The panel should explore one coordinated intake, routing, benefits explanation, and reporting surface.
