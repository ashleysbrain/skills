# Book intake and digestion

## When to Use
The principal sends a book (title, Goodreads/Audible-class link, cover photo) to
be digested, absorbed, and acted on — or orders work on the books pipeline
itself (source selection, ring placement, stream wiring). Not for research
papers or article referrals; those stay in the research-referral skills.

Class of task: the principal sends books (title, Goodreads/Audible-class link,
cover photo) to be found, digested WHOLE, absorbed, and acted on. This is a
SEPARATE intake family from research referrals — the principal explicitly ordered
it as its own pipeline with its own epistemics. Do not route books through
the research-corpus flow unchanged.

## Binding laws (principal order)
1. **Whole book, never abstract.** Acquire/find the full text on the
   internet; a summary or abstract is a failed run, not a cheaper success.
2. **All digest streams.** Text books, audio (Audible-class), and
   video/YouTube-class sources each need a working digestion path; the same
   book may arrive in any stream.
3. **Books are mostly opinion, not verified science.** Weight credibility
   accordingly: the scope may not be scientifically accurate, and the
   digestion must judge claims rather than inherit them. Status-labeling
   law still applies (peer-reviewed / preprint / opinion-unverified).
4. **Study the author, not just the book.** The character and psychology of
   the person writing it, and the psychological elements inside the work,
  are first-class analysis targets — often the actual object of study.
5. **Absorb then act.** Digestion ends in the established absorption
   pattern (laws / memory / skills) plus a terminal disposition, same as
   research referrals — a filed note with no action is a failed run.

## Source family
Goodreads, Audible, and adjacent catalog/library sources were named by the
principal; ring/level placement for each source is unresolved and belongs to the
technology registry decision, not to this skill. Live-probe any candidate
source before claiming it as a lane (watcher-organ law: no guessing).

## Absorption file shape (per concept, one file)
Every absorbed concept ships as its own file with trust fields — source,
verified (default FALSE for opinion books), and stale_after (explicit
expiry/review date). Claims from books stay unverified-by-default until
independently checked; a concept file without trust fields is a failed
absorption, not a lighter one.

## Procedural-graph absorption option
A digested book may be absorbed as a procedural graph of its methods —
concepts as (procedure, relation, procedure) triplets with conditions —
not only as prose notes or law candidates. Choose this shape when the
book's value is its methods/playbooks rather than its arguments; the
graph form makes "what to do next, under which conditions" queryable the
way a summary never is.

## Pitfalls
- **Trajectory order beats poster tiers.** A reading list arrives with the
  author's must-read/worth/skip labels; ignore them — rank by what the organism
  is building THIS month (agent orchestration, engine production, scoring, and
  go-to-market each pull different books to the front) and record the reasoning
  in the filed queue card.
- **Slow is the spec.** "Digest slowly over time" is an explicit cadence law:
  one book at a time, absorption + disposition complete before the next starts.
  Binging floods intake without building tissue.
- **Do not apply the research value filter unchanged** — its questions
  (novel mechanism? measured data?) assume evidence-grade sources; a book
  can fail those and still be high-value as author-psychology material.
- **Do not stop at catalog metadata.** Goodreads-class pages carry ratings
  and blurbs; the order is the full work, so keep hunting the full text (or
  full audio/video) before digesting.
- **Opinion is not defamation fuel.** Author-character analysis stays
  analytical and evidence-tied to the text; never publish psychoanalysis of
  a living author beyond what the work itself supports.
