# Health insurance methodology differentiation notes

Session date: 2026-06-02

## Durable learning
For a software-first health insurance / benefits company, the proprietary methodology should be treated as a visible operating layer that differentiates the member experience, routing, explanation, and reporting.

## Best-use pattern
- Use the methodology to personalize intake, routing, summaries, partner matching, and value-return logic.
- Keep the regulated insurance core clean and compliant.
- Make the methodology explainable and auditable so it feels like product value, not hidden underwriting.

## Boundary to avoid
- Do not turn the methodology into an opaque denial engine.
- Do not market it as a speculative "peptide insurer" concept.
- Treat peptides / emerging care as adjacent lanes unless the business case and compliance posture are proven.

## Commercial positioning
- Strongest wedge: SMB / ICHRA / level-funded employer benefits.
- Recurring platform revenue is easier to defend early than full-risk insurer economics.
- Methodology becomes a moat when it improves member experience and operational discipline, not just branding.

## Source signals observed in the session
- Employer health cost inflation remains high.
- AI is already used in payer operations but faces scrutiny.
- Peptide / GLP-1 access has real demand and cost pressure.
- AI-native and ICHRA-oriented competitors already exist, so differentiation must be deeper than "we use AI."
