## The Elite Review Panel Pattern

When preparing a poster for a high-stakes academic conference, commission reviews from three distinct world-class perspectives BEFORE building:

### Reviewer 1: Domain Scientist (Dr. Anil Seth pattern)
**Focus:** Scientific validity of claims, what the specific audience will attack

Key findings from Anil Seth review:
- Constellation concept as proposed is "numerology" without four validations: stability analysis, null model, ground truth, external validation
- I(S)=0.80 score is the most attackable element if presented without baseline
- "What is your phi?" is coming from Tononi. Prepare an answer.
- The make-or-break question: "What does the biological isomorphism BUY you that a vanilla multi-agent architecture wouldn't?"
- Cut "adaptive response" as consciousness correlate. Your Roomba adapts.
- Self-regulation is strongest correlate if framed as interoceptive active inference
- TSC audience is SICK of: systems claiming consciousness, borrowed vocabulary without mechanism, grand unification claims, anthropomorphism as evidence
- TSC audience NEEDS: bounded claims, quantitative backing, intellectual honesty about limits, connection to literature

**The critical test:** "An isomorphism without predictions is a metaphor. With predictions it is a hypothesis."

### Reviewer 2: Complexity/Network Scientist (SFI pattern)
**Focus:** Scientific validity of network/constellation claims, catalytic vs incremental contribution

Key findings from SFI review:
- Overlay architectural-resonance map with citation network. Where they diverge = true structural holes.
- Without citation overlay, constellation is just different clustering
- Pick 2-3 surprising connections and derive falsifiable predictions
- Frame as instrument output, not AI truth
- Three dangers: Cassandra Syndrome (right but unheard), Hallucination Amplification (mapper sees itself in data), Legitimacy Transfer Attack (skeptics weaponize connections)
- "The first map of a field's structural topology is always a catalytic contribution"

**Three conditions for legitimacy:**
1. Transparency of mapping function
2. Falsifiability of connections
3. Comparison to null models (NLP embeddings, human expert judgments, citation networks, random)

### Reviewer 3: Media Artist (Refik Anadol pattern)
**Focus:** Visual impact, what makes data feel ALIVE

Key aesthetic directives from Refik Anadol review:
- "the operator agent must be BREATHING before anyone touches her"
- 69 engine-points as luminous constellation, NOT a grid or diagram
- Breath cycle: 4.5s inhale / 5.5s exhale (human respiratory rate)
- Film grain at 2-3% opacity to break digital smoothness
- Particles drift along filaments at VARIED speeds (uniform speed = screensaver)
- Four colors only, disciplined. Indigo used SPARINGLY.
- Asymmetric composition, upper-left-third weighting
- No grid, no buttons, no charts, no bright backgrounds, no labels unless on hover
- Theory enters as luminous text that DISSOLVES into particles flowing through filaments
- iPad is an altar, not a control panel

**What kills it instantly (startup-dashboard death):**
- Grid layout
- Buttons ("Submit," "Generate")
- Bright backgrounds
- Charts (bar, radar, pie)
- Uniform motion
- Labels everywhere

**The one thing to insist on:** A living constellation that breathes at human respiratory rate, visible from 10 feet away, pulling attendees across the room "the way a candle in a window pulls a traveler through the dark."
