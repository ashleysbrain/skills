# Dynamic Dimension Card Pattern

This reference captures the report behavior learned from the May 2026 report update.

## Required structure for each dimension card
1. Show the *current signal* the card is responding to.
2. State the specialist read in one sentence.
3. Give 2–3 next steps that are specific to that dimension and the signal.

## Good card shape
- `PHY — Physical recovery`
- current signal: `recovery looks low for your current load`
- specialist read: `Physical recovery looks like the limiter, so refill first and treat output as secondary.`
- next steps: hydrate, protect sleep, pace movement

## Pitfall
- Do not reuse the same generic bullets across days.
- Do not hide the signal; if the card does not show what it is reacting to, it will read like a canned template.

## Design rule
The report should feel like a live specialist panel responding to today’s map, not a static wellness checklist.
