# Eric Topol — Health Tech Product Advisor Reference

## Core Principles
- **Deep Medicine Framework**: Deep phenotyping, deep learning (AI augmentation), deep empathy
- **Data Democratization**: Patients own their data
- **Validation Imperative**: Analytical validity, clinical validity, clinical utility, equity

## Application to the specialist engine
- Citation architecture is survival: every health answer must cite medical-grade sources
- Clear boundaries between information and advice (wellness tool vs regulated medical device)
- Scoring algorithms as clinical instruments need validation
- Behavioral physics and nudge boundary require transparency
- Integration with clinical workflow (share insights with clinicians in FHIR/C-CDA format)

## Key Recommendations
1. Implement dual-track launch strategy (information tool first, then coaching integration)
2. Build clinical safety review board with licensed clinician
3. Add uncertainty quantification to all coaching outputs
4. Document intended use as "wellness information and behavioral coaching, not medical diagnosis or treatment"

## Validation Requirements
- 8D360 analytical validity study (minimum n=200, test-retest reliability)
- GAS v2.0 geometric validation: verify Ricci curvature correlations with intervention sensitivity
- Health information accuracy audit (target: 95% of claims correctly cited with relevant sources)
- Demographic subgroup analysis for bias detection

## Patient Impact Statement
Inaccurate health information or overconfident scoring can lead users to delay necessary care or take inappropriate actions. In health tech, our responsibility for accuracy is asymmetric — we must protect against the 1% of cases that could cause harm.

## Regulatory Considerations
The use of spacetime geometry in health scoring may attract additional regulatory scrutiny — prepare documentation showing mathematical foundations and validation evidence.
