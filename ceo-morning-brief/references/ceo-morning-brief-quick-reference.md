# principal Morning Brief Quick Reference

A concise checklist for conducting the the specialist engine principal Advisory Board's morning review.

## 📋 PRE-SESSION PREPARATION
- [ ] Locate and review `COMMITMENTS.md`
- [ ] Gather all upstream panel outputs (Marketing, Product, CTO, COO, Design)
- [ ] Check for overnight developments or file modifications
- [ ] Clear distractions and prepare for focused work

## 📝 MORNING BRIEF STRUCTURE
Use this exact format:

---
## principal Morning Brief — [DATE]

### THE ONE THING
[One concrete sentence - single highest priority]

### TODAY'S PRIORITY STACK (ranked)
1. **[ACTION]** — Impact: [why] — Source: [advisor] — Time: [estimate]
2. **[ACTION]** — Impact: [why] — Source: [advisor] — Time: [estimate]
3. [3-5 items MAX]

### WHAT'S OFF THE LIST (and why)
- [Item] — [why it can wait]
- [Item] — [why it can wait]

### COMMITMENT CHECK
[Specify updates to COMMITMENTS.md]

### DECISIONS NEEDED FROM ASHLEY
- **[DECISION]**: What to decide — Blocks: [what's blocked] — Default: [recommendation]

---

## ✅ VERIFICATION CHECKLIST
- [ ] Format matches exactly (triple dashes, section headers)
- [ ] "THE ONE THING" = exactly one sentence
- [ ] Priority stack = 3-5 items, each with Impact/Source/Time
- [ ] Each priority item correctly identifies source panel
- [ ] "WHAT'S OFF THE LIST" explains deferral reasons
- [ ] COMMITMENTS update instructions are clear
- [ ] Decisions listed are ONLY blocking decisions
- [ ] NO AI telltales (em-dashes, "Furthermore/Moreover")
- [ ] NO vague or abstract language
- [ ] ALL content is concrete and actionable

## 🚨 COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID
- ❌ Vague actions ("review strategy", "consider options")
- ❌ More than 5 priority items (overwhelms ADHD focus)
- ❌ Missing source identification for actions
- ❌ Forgetting to update COMMITMENTS.md
- ❌ Including non-blocking decisions
- ❌ Using corporate jargon or abstraction
- ❌ Making "THE ONE THING" compound/complex
- ❌ Not explaining why deferred items can wait

## 🎯 SOURCE IDENTIFICATION QUICK GUIDE
- **Marketing**: Positioning, messaging, launch plans, audience targeting
- **Product**: Opportunity trees, user research, feature specs, outcomes
- **CTO**: Tech architecture, engineering priorities, infrastructure, security
- **COO**: Operational processes, resource allocation, workflow optimization
- **Design**: UX flows, visual design, brand identity, interaction details
- **principal**: the principal's direct statements, strategic decisions, personal commitments

## ⏱️ TIME ESTIMATE GUIDE
- 5-15 min: Quick decisions, communications, brief reviews
- 30 min: Meetings, document reviews, simple approvals
- 45-60 min: Deep work, analysis, preparation
- 2+ hours: Complex tasks requiring sustained focus
- Half day: Major deliverables, significant work blocks

## 💡 REMINDERS
- The goal is to tell the principal EXACTLY what to work on today, in priority order
- Give explicit permission to NOT do certain things (what's off the list)
- Respect ADHD needs: concrete > abstract, prioritized > exhaustive
- Never include abstract suggestions - every item must be actionable today
- If in doubt, go more specific and concrete
