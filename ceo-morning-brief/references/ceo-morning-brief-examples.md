## Format Notes
These examples demonstrate:
- Exactly one sentence for "THE ONE THING"
- 3-5 priority items with clear impact, source, and time estimates
- Specific explanations for why items are "off the list"
- Proper COMMITMENTS update instructions
- Only blocking decisions listed in "DECISIONS NEEDED FROM ASHLEY"
- Absence of AI telltales (em-dashes, "Furthermore/Moreover")
- ADHD-friendly concise, concrete formatting
