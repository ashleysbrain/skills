## Specific Aims

- **Aim 1 (Mo 1–6):** Lock human parameterization of the 6+1 engines — COG
  (information theory/MDL), PIPE (queueing M/G/1), RES (stochastic
  thermodynamics), KNOW (non-memoryless decay), ALIGN (Landau–Ginzburg
  barriers), ADAPT (relaxation/CSD early warning), INTER (Kuramoto coherence,
  conditional 7th) — on retrospective telemetry via shadow-scoring sandbox
  with 30-day promotion rule.
- **Aim 2 (Mo 4–12):** Prospective validation cohort with academic partner;
  test–retest reliability, discriminant validity vs. gold-standard
  instruments. Minimal-risk observational; IRB through partner institution.
- **Aim 3 (Mo 7–12):** Validate composite Bloch-sphere state score (drift
  magnitude θ, direction φ, decisiveness r, deliberation t_c) against scalar
  baselines; publish detection SLA ("given drift δ, detect with probability p
  within T days").

**Significance:** every existing instrument returns a static scalar snapshot;
none measures health as a trajectory or predicts degradation.
**Innovation:** measurement math from fundamental physics — Fisher–Rao
metric unifies all drift signals; participation ratio sets per-person
effective dimensionality; geometric-mean composite prevents compensatory
masking. **Approach:** GO/NO-GO gates at engine lock (Mo 6) and cohort
enrollment threshold (Mo 8).

**Researchers:** PD/PI = principal (instrument architect). Key personnel: Dr.
Anantua (physics review of engine derivations), Dr. Alterovitz (validation
design). Subaward: Gilchrist Research — VERITAS pipeline hardening, cohort
data security, ring-topology endpoint coordination.

## Institute targeting

- **NIBIB — primary.** Bioengineering/measurement instrumentation mandate;
  named physics reads native; Aim 3 state geometry is the "device" claim.
- **NIMH — secondary.** Measurement-based care; ALIGN/ADAPT behavioral
  trajectories.
- **NINDS — credible** if cohort oversamples neurodegenerative decline; CSD
  is tipping-point detection.
- **NIDA — viable** for relapse-precursor monitoring.
- Recommendation: NIBIB parent omnibus; hold NIMH as re-target. The physics
  is disease-agnostic — institute choice is a cohort-recruitment choice.

## NSF Seed Fund

Gate 1 Project Pitch: (a) innovation — physics scoring engines as universal
health-state estimators; (b) market — measurement/certification layer for
digital health; (c) societal impact — earlier decline detection. Gate 2 Full
Proposal tech paragraph: fundamental-science basis → delta on state of art →
technical objectives = cohort validation milestones → commercialization
(API, certification, private instances). Frame as deep tech:
fundamental-science-based and internally validated, not exploratory.

## Cohort as federal asset

One cohort, two applications, three functions: NIH feasibility data (Phase II
competitiveness), NSF technical-risk reduction, #1 credibility asset.
Design once, cite twice: pre-register, power for primary endpoint, lock
analysis plan before enrollment. The cohort is THE milestone converting
*theoretical → in-validation*: engines exist, instrument exists; humans are
what remain.
