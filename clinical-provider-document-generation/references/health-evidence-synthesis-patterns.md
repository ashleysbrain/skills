# Health Evidence Synthesis Patterns

Use this pattern when a research or methodology skill is being adapted into a health-facing product.

## Three conversion modes

1. **Health Methodology Review and Consolidation**
   - Best as the public core.
   - Reviews health research, drafts, and protocols.
   - Converts messy inputs into a bounded, implementation-ready, non-diagnostic health spec.

2. **Health Pattern Review for Patient 0**
   - For personal health signals, journaling, transcripts, and wearable trends.
   - Summarizes patterns and confounders without diagnosing.
   - Separates observed pattern from interpretation.

3. **Provider-Ready Health Brief Builder**
   - Turns health evidence into a concise provider handoff.
   - Emphasizes facts, confidence, limitations, and questions for the clinician.
   - No diagnostic language.

## Claim-status labels

Use these labels consistently when health evidence is incomplete:
- validated
- supported but incomplete
- hypothesis
- provisional
- must not ship

## Conversion rule

If the underlying method is not yet safe to present as a health skill, keep the public surface to synthesis and protocol hardening only. Avoid diagnosis, treatment directives, or medical certainty.
