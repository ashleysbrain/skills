# University Philosophy Stacking for Agent Personas

Use this reference when the user wants more than prestige shorthand and is asking for the *philosophy of the brain*.

## Goal
Turn credentials into an inspectable architecture:
- school
- field / concentration
- methodological contribution
- why it belongs in this persona
- what blind spot it offsets

## Required rendering shape
For each school in the stack, write:
1. **University + degree / field**
2. **Why this school is in the stack**
3. **Philosophy contribution**
4. **Why it matters for this mission**
5. **What it offsets in the rest of the stack**

## Common philosophy anchors
These are examples, not a fixed canon:

- **Stanford** — product-systems leverage, scalable primitives, strategic architecture under shipping pressure
- **MIT** — systems rigor, optimization, queueing, formal operations logic, measurable bottlenecks
- **Carnegie Mellon** — HCI realism, bounded rationality, operator-aware workflows, cognitive fit
- **Berkeley** — information systems, open-systems realism, infrastructure truthfulness, semantic rigor
- **Georgia Tech** — systems engineering, decomposition discipline, control points, interface contracts
- **Wharton / MIT Sloan** — organizational design, management science, decision-making under uncertainty
- **Cambridge / Oxford** — psychometrics, statistics, historical pattern analysis, evidence discipline
- **Chicago** — process philosophy, temporal logic, identity across change
- **UIUC** — taxonomy, retrieval, controlled vocabularies, information stewardship
- **ETH Zurich** — implementation-grade engineering precision

## Output reminder
Do not stop at:
- "PhD in Computer Science"

Instead say what the school contributes, e.g.:
- Stanford CS for product-aware platform judgment
- MIT systems for optimization rigor
- CMU HCI for human-in-the-loop workflow design

## Auditability rule
If some of the lineage is verified on disk and some is a designed overlay:
- label the verified part explicitly
- label the designed part explicitly
- never blur the boundary
