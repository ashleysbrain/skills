# Warm coaching-question tone

Session lesson: when the user asks for more user-friendly or psychologically fitting coaching questions, keep the question flow warm, validating, and agency-preserving.

The coaching voice should also use principal language only as a metaphor for taking charge of life. Do not make the copy sound like it is designed only for CEOs; keep it broadly accessible and human.

## Good patterns
- What’s on your mind today?
- What would feel most helpful right now?
- What would you like to get unstuck on first?
- What would a good outcome look like for you?
- What feels blocked right now?

## Avoid
- clinical intake language
- interrogative or exam-like framing
- phrasing that makes the user feel analyzed instead of supported

## Rule of thumb
If a question can be made softer without losing clarity, make it softer.
