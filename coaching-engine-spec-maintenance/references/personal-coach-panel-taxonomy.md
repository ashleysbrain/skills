# Personal Coach Panel Taxonomy

Session note: the user's personal coach panel is distinct from the general engine taxonomy.

Use this panel shape when building a coach panel for the user:
- the specialist engine: stable synthesis role
- Industry-matched principal mirror: real public principal from the user's target industry
- Marketing principal mirror: real public growth / brand / distribution operator
- Finance / funding principal mirror: real public capital / monetization operator
- Talent principal mirror: real public people / org / hiring operator
- Growth principal mirror: real public scale / acquisition operator
- Health / medical principal mirror: real public health-tech / medical operator
- Mission / philosophy alignment agent: a philosophy or operating doctrine, not necessarily a human unless a good human is found

Selection rule:
- This is for the user's personal coach panel, not the general engine or a universal roster.
- Professional seats should be mapped to public humans.
- Personal or identity seats should be mapped to philosophies or frameworks.
